# Deferred register

Every obligation a terminal plan parked, in one place. Rows are self-sufficient — the condensed
claim, why it matters, and the revisit condition live here, because source plans are retired.
Provenance points into Git history, not the working tree. `Disposition` is the lift's reason tag —
`out-of-scope | needs-decision | cost | blocked-by` — or `accepted gap`: one taxonomy from plan
to register.
